# Material care

**Leather:** wipe with a damp cloth, let dry at room temperature, and condition every 2 to 3 months. Never put leather in a washing machine.

**Suede:** brush with a suede brush when dry. Use a suede eraser for marks. Apply a water-repellent spray before first wear.

**Mesh:** hand wash with mild soap and cold water. Remove the insoles and air dry away from direct sunlight. Do not tumble dry.
