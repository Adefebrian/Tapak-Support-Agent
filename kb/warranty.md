# Warranty

All Tapak shoes have a 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects, counted from the delivery date.

- Covered: sole separation, broken stitching, faulty eyelets, and defective zippers.
- Not covered: normal wear, damage from misuse, and damage from incorrect care.
- Warranty claims require photos and are assessed by the support team. The outcome can be repair, replacement, or store credit.
