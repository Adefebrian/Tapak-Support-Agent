# Tapak Points

Every IDR 10,000 spent earns 1 Tapak Point. Points are added 14 days after delivery, once the return window has mostly passed.

- 100 points can be exchanged for a IDR 50,000 voucher.
- Points expire 12 months after they are earned.
- Points are removed for items that are returned.
- Members get early access to new releases and a birthday voucher.
