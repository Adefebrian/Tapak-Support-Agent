# International shipping

We currently ship to Singapore and Malaysia only.

- Delivery takes 5 to 10 business days after dispatch.
- Shipping costs IDR 150,000 per order. Import duties and taxes are paid by the customer on arrival.
- International orders can be sent back within 30 days of delivery. The customer pays the postage.
- We do not ship to other countries yet.
