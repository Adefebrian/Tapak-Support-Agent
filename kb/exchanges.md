# Size exchanges

We offer one free size exchange per pair within 30 days of delivery.

- The shoes must be unworn and in the original box.
- Exchanges depend on stock of the new size. If the size is out of stock, we offer a refund instead.
- A size exchange is handled by the support team, who will send a prepaid label and reserve the new size.
- Mixed-size orders can be exchanged per item.

Use our size guide (kb-size-006) before requesting an exchange.
