# Shipping times

Orders are packed within 1 business day after payment is confirmed.

| Region | Estimated delivery after dispatch |
| --- | --- |
| Jabodetabek | 1 to 2 business days |
| Java (outside Jabodetabek) | 2 to 4 business days |
| Outside Java | 3 to 7 business days |

Shipping is free for orders above IDR 500,000. Below that, a flat fee of IDR 25,000 applies. If an order has not arrived 5 business days after its estimated date, support will open a trace with the carrier.
