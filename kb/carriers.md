# Carriers and tracking

We ship with JNE, SiCepat, and J&T. The carrier is chosen automatically based on your region.

- A tracking number is issued when your order status changes to "shipped".
- Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.
- If a carrier marks a parcel as lost, support files a claim and offers a replacement or a refund. This decision is made by support staff.
