# Contacting support

Our human support team works Monday to Saturday, 09:00 to 18:00 WIB.

- Requests escalated by the assistant are answered within 1 business day.
- High-priority cases (payment disputes, legal matters, safety issues) are reviewed within 4 business hours.
- Support can issue refunds, approve exchanges, change addresses before dispatch, and approve policy exceptions.
