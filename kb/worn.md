# Worn or used items

Shoes that show signs of outdoor wear (dirty soles, creased uppers, scuffs) cannot be returned or exchanged.

Trying shoes on indoors on a clean surface does not count as wear. If you are unsure whether your pair qualifies, a support staff member can review photos. Manufacturing defects on worn shoes are covered by the warranty (kb-warranty-008), not by the return policy.
