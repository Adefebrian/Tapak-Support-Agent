# Choosing a shoe by activity

This guide matches our models to everyday activities. It is product information, not medical advice. For foot pain or a medical condition, please see a podiatrist.

- **Walking all day or commuting:** Langkah Runner. Cushioned sole, breathable mesh, removable insole.
- **Running up to 10 km:** Langkah Runner. For longer distances, a dedicated running shop can fit you.
- **Gym and indoor training:** Angin Mesh Trainer. Flat, stable sole and light weight.
- **Office:** Kulit Derby. Leather models run slightly narrow, so consider half a size up.
- **Weekend and travel:** Senja Suede Loafer, or Pantai Sandal for the beach.
- **Hiking and rain:** Gunung Boot, our only water-resistant model.

All adult models have a removable insole, so you can use your own insoles.
