# Payment methods

We accept bank transfer (virtual account), credit and debit cards, QRIS, and e-wallets (GoPay, OVO, DANA).

- Virtual account payments must be completed within 24 hours or the order is cancelled automatically.
- Payment confirmation usually takes under 10 minutes.
- We never ask for your card number, PIN, or OTP over chat.
