# Gift cards

Tapak gift cards come in IDR 250,000, 500,000, and 1,000,000.

- Gift cards are sent by email and are valid for 12 months from purchase.
- A gift card can be combined with one voucher on the same order.
- Unused balance stays on the card until it expires. It cannot be exchanged for cash.
- Lost gift card codes can be resent by support to the email that bought them.
