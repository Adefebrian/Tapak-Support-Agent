# Stores and pickup

Tapak has three stores, all in Indonesia, open every day from 10:00 to 21:00 WIB:

- Jakarta, Senayan
- Bandung, Dago
- Surabaya, Tunjungan

We have no stores outside Indonesia.

- Online orders can be picked up for free in any store. Choose "Store pickup" at checkout. The order is ready within 2 business days.
- Online orders can be returned in a store within the normal 30-day window. Bring the order ID.
