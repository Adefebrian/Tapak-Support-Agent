# Damaged, wrong, or missing items

If your shoes arrive damaged, in the wrong size or model, or with an item missing, report it within 7 days of delivery.

- Send your order ID and photos of the item, the box, and the shipping label.
- Support replaces the item or refunds it in full, and return shipping is free.
- You do not need to wait for the 30-day return window to start. Wrong or damaged items are handled first.

The assistant can explain these steps, but a support staff member reviews the photos and decides the outcome.
