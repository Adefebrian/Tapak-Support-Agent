# Pre-orders

Pre-order items ship on the date shown on the product page, usually 3 to 6 weeks after ordering.

- Pre-orders are paid in full at checkout.
- A pre-order can be cancelled for a full refund until it ships. Cancellation is handled by support staff.
- If a pre-order is delayed by more than 2 weeks, we notify you by email.
