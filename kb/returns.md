# Return policy

You can return unworn shoes within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method.

- Shoes must be unworn, in the original box, with all tags attached.
- The 30 days start on the delivery date shown in your tracking, not the order date.
- Refunds are processed by our support team within 5 to 7 business days after the return arrives at our warehouse.
- Return shipping is free for orders inside Indonesia using the prepaid label sent by support.
- Returns after 30 days are not accepted automatically. Any exception must be reviewed by a support staff member.

To start a return, contact support with your order ID. The assistant cannot issue refunds itself.
