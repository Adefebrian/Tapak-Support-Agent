# Finding and changing your order

Your order ID starts with TPK- followed by 5 digits. It is in the subject line of the order confirmation email and on the packing slip.

- To check an order status in this chat, give the order ID and the email address used at checkout.
- Changes before the order ships (address, size, adding an item) are made by the support team, not by the assistant. Ask for a person and include your order ID.
- After an order has shipped it cannot be changed. You can return or exchange it after delivery.
